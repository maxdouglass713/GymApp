# Logo Setup Instructions

## ✅ Ready to Use Your Logo!

Your app is now configured to use your custom logo image. Here's what you need to do:

### Step 1: Add Your Logo Image
1. **Save your logo** as `logo.png`
2. **Place it in**: `kineticflowai/assets/images/logo.png`

### Step 2: That's It!
The app will automatically use your logo image instead of the generated placeholder.

## What's Been Updated:
- ✅ **Welcome Screen** - Uses your logo image
- ✅ **Home Screen** - Uses your logo image  
- ✅ **Logo Component** - Configured for your image
- ✅ **Image sizing** - Optimized for your logo
- ✅ **Transparent background** - Shows your logo's black background

## Logo Requirements:
- **Format**: PNG (recommended)
- **Size**: Any size (will be scaled automatically)
- **Background**: Your logo already has the perfect black background

## Fallback:
If the image file is missing, the app will show the generated brain+dumbbell logo as a fallback.
