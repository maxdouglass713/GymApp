**Last Updated:** November 24, 2025

## Introduction

KINETIC FLOW AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

### Information You Provide

- **Health & Fitness Data:** Workout logs, exercise data, nutrition information, weight tracking, and fitness goals
- **Profile Information:** Height, weight, age, fitness experience, goals, and preferences
- **Usage Data:** Features you use, points earned, and app interactions

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, and app version
- **Usage Analytics:** How you interact with the app (features used, session duration)
- **Technical Data:** Crash reports and performance metrics

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide and improve our fitness tracking services
